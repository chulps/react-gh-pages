Before you get in touch

Good questions.
Straight answers.

I received an email from you. What happens now?

You can look through the services and real work here, then reply to the email you received or contact me directly. Share your website URL and what you want to improve. We start by clarifying the problem.

Can you work on my existing website?

Yes. A focused repair or improvement may be all you need. I review your current site and setup before recommending changes, including whether a rebuild is justified.

Can you help with PHP errors or a broken form?

I can investigate the issue and assess the work required. The right fix depends on the code, hosting and any plugins involved. Send the page URL and describe what went wrong; please do not email passwords.

How much will it cost?

The cost depends on the problem, access and agreed scope. I’ll ask questions and confirm the scope and estimate in writing before work starts. There is no obligation to commission a rebuild.

Will SEO changes guarantee a higher Google ranking?

No. Search rankings cannot be guaranteed. I can review technical and on-page basics, make useful improvements and agree how to check the result.

Can we communicate in Japanese?

Japanese inquiries are welcome by email with translation support. I do not offer Japanese-language phone calls.

What should I send first?

Your website URL, a short description of the issue, and any timing constraints. For an internal tool, describe the task you want to make easier. You do not need a technical brief.
