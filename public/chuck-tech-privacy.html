Chuck Tech — Privacy Policy

Effective: October 27, 2026

About Chuck Tech

Chuck Tech develops private website analytics, SEO monitoring, and conversion optimization tools.

Google Data Access

With account authorization, Chuck Tech accesses read-only Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and BigQuery data for website performance analysis.

Data Usage

Data is used to monitor search performance, evaluate A/B experiments, and generate website optimization recommendations. This may include pseudonymous visitor identifiers and event data.

Data Storage and Sharing

Authentication tokens and analytics exports are stored in operator-controlled environments. Aggregated reporting data may be processed by third-party AI services. Raw visitor-level experiment records are not intentionally sent to AI services.

Google user data is not sold or used for targeted advertising. Records are retained for reporting purposes until no longer needed or deleted by the operator.

Security and Access

Access is restricted to authorized accounts. Google permissions can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
